IQ Tests and the Concept of Intelligence

**IQ (Intelligence Quotient)** is the result of standardized tests aiming to measure a person's cognitive abilities. These tests typically assess skills in areas such as logic, problem-solving, spatial reasoning, verbal reasoning, and memory. However, intelligence is a complex and multifaceted concept, and IQ tests measure only one aspect of intelligence.
