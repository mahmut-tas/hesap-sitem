AdSense Revenue Estimation

AdSense Revenue: Cost Per Click (CPC) and Revenue Per Mille (RPM)

Google AdSense provides publishers with revenue through ad impressions and ad clicks. It is important to understand these two main revenue sources:

  • Cost Per Click (CPC): The revenue you earn when visitors click on an ad. CPC values vary greatly depending on advertiser bids, niche, and competition.
  • Revenue Per Mille (RPM): The estimated revenue you earn for every 1000 ad impressions. This includes both clicks (if any) and impression-based payments, indicating how effectively a publisher's website is generating revenue.

How is the Calculation Made?

This calculator estimates your revenue from two main components, using a hypothetical **average CPC** and **average RPM** value for your selected region and category:
Monthly Click Revenue = (Daily Clicks * Average CPC) * 30 Days
Monthly Impression Revenue = (Monthly Page Views / 1000) * Average RPM
Total Estimated Monthly Revenue = Monthly Click Revenue + Monthly Impression Revenue

The CPC and RPM coefficients in this calculator are hypothetical values based on market generalizations and do not reflect Google's real-time data or complex algorithms. Your actual AdSense revenue may differ significantly from these estimates due to many dynamic factors such as advertiser demand, seasonal trends, ad placement, user behavior, and ad-blocking software. For the most accurate data, it is always recommended to check the performance reports in your own Google AdSense account.
